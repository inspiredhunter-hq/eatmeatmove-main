STRONG Decision Framework™

A simpler way to think about food.

The STRONG Decision Framework™ gives you six simple questions to help you choose food that supports strength, satisfaction and everyday life.

S

Stop Naturally

Will this help me feel satisfied enough to stop eating without relying on willpower?

T

Training Support

Will this support my movement, recovery and strength?

R

Real Food

Did this come mainly from a farm or has it been heavily engineered in a factory?

O

Optimal Protein

Does this give me enough high-quality protein to support muscle and recovery?

N

Next Meal

Will I stay satisfied until I actually want or need to eat again?

G

Glucose Response

How is this likely to affect my energy and hunger afterwards?

No points. No complicated rules. Just six better questions.
